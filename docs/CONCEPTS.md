# Concept selection

Research reviewed Phaser touch/scaling documentation, Google AdMob natural-break and test-ad guidance, GitHub Pages workflows and Capacitor Android support. Ratings below are design estimates, not measured retention or revenue.

| Concept | Core decision | Replayability | Retention hypothesis | Development cost | Advertising fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Orbit Shift | Time changes between two orbital lanes | High | Timing mastery, chain scoring and best scores | Low, procedural gates and vector art | Round breaks and optional rescue |
| Pocket Stacker | Release moving blocks to stack a tower | Medium | Height and perfect placements | Low | Natural failures; rescue can weaken the skill loop |
| Comet Courier | Drag a ship between three lanes to collect parcels | High | Route mastery and collection | Medium, more patterns and balancing | Natural failures and optional rescue |

Selected: **Orbit Shift**. It offers simple input with predictable geometry, readable hazards and varied safe-lane patterns, with little authored content to maintain. The other two remain distinct future candidates. This is an original implementation, not a claim that the genre is novel. Trademark availability and store saturation need release-stage review.

Target: voluntary replays, immediate controls, fair gaps and modest audiovisual feedback. Timing and difficulty are playtest hypotheses. The current event recorder stays in memory; return visits and cohort retention need a later consent-reviewed analytics integration.
