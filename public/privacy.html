Orbit Shift development privacy notice

The browser game saves your best score, sound choice and round count locally. Gameplay events remain in temporary memory and are not sent to an analytics server. Clear these using browser site-data controls.

The standalone browser game requests no advertising. GitHub Pages hosts this edition and may process connection data under its own privacy statement. Android test builds include Google Mobile Ads and UMP consent with Google demo IDs; these SDKs have their own data practices.

This development notice is not the final publisher privacy policy. Publisher contact, audience/country review, SDK inventory and Data safety disclosures are required before store release or production advertising.
